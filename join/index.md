# {% include icon.html icon="fa-solid fa-user-graduate" %}Prospective Students

We welcome applications from students and postdocs with backgrounds in physics, engineering, computer science, neuroscience, kinesiology or medicine who want to understand how the heart and the brain age together.

Projects in the lab combine MRI physics and pulse-sequence work, image processing and machine learning, and clinical studies with real participants, so there is room for people who like to build methods as well as those who want to answer physiological questions.

{% include section.html %}

## How to apply

Send Dr. Gauthier an email with:

- your CV and unofficial transcripts
- a few sentences on your research interests and which of our [projects](../projects) appeal to you
- the degree you are applying for (MSc, PhD, postdoc or internship) and your intended start date
