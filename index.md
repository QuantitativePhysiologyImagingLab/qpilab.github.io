# What does the heart reveal about the brain?

The **Quantitative Physiological Imaging Lab** develops and applies quantitative MRI to measure the vascular and metabolic health of the brain: blood flow, the ability of vessels to dilate, their pulsatility, and how much oxygen the brain extracts. These measures can change long before the damage seen with conventional imaging.

We study how aging and cardiovascular disease transform brain health, with one goal: identify early the people whose brain is more vulnerable to aging, and find out how to act before the damage becomes irreversible.

The lab is led by [Dr. Claudine Gauthier](members/claudine-gauthier) at the [Université de Montréal](https://www.umontreal.ca), and is affiliated with the [Montreal Heart Institute](https://www.icm-mhi.org) Research Centre and the [EPIC Centre](https://www.centreepic.org).

## Our approach

{% capture col1 %}
### One scan, many measures
In a single one-hour MRI session we map cerebral blood flow, cerebrovascular reactivity, pulsatility, oxygen extraction fraction and myelin, some of which change early in aging and others later.
{% endcapture %}

{% capture col2 %}
### From differences to trajectories
With computational models and AI, we build vulnerability profiles from these measures to understand which differences predict future cognitive decline.
{% endcapture %}

{% capture col3 %}
### Intervening before decline
Through trials such as NeuroDance, we test whether exercise, dance and cognitive training can change the trajectory of brain aging.
{% endcapture %}
